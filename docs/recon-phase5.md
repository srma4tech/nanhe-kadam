# Phase 5 reconnaissance — review timing and family rhythm

The schedule already defines a 20-minute full session, 10-minute Friday/Saturday light sessions, one default session per enabled day, and Sunday as an adventure card. The session engine persists only its active step; activity answers are not stored and no mastery/review planner exists.

Implement a pure mastery/review planner with levels 0–3 and intervals of 1, 3, 7, and 21 days. Keep due reviews small (at most six prompts or five minutes), bias toward weaker/due concepts while adding a familiar easy prompt and a weekly theme touch. Retain Saturday as the weekly recap slot and add a monthly Gaon Mela adventure card without enabling Sunday games. Parent summaries should use aggregate local counts and avoid rankings or pressure.

Automated date/mode simulation can verify interval and cap behavior; real family pacing and the suitability of the 5-minute cap require a pilot and cannot be verified in this workspace.
