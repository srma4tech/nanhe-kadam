# Curriculum human review checklist

Use this checklist with a Hindi and English speaking adult before showing the curriculum to children. Automated schema checks do not replace this review.

- [ ] Read all 28 activities, missions, story summaries, and paper prompts aloud in both languages.
- [ ] Check that Hindi is natural and age appropriate and that English is simple and idiomatic.
- [ ] Confirm each week reinforces familiar basics while introducing only a small new idea.
- [ ] Confirm every activity is supervised, uses safe household materials, and never asks children to go outside alone, taste/handle unknown items, or share private information.
- [ ] Confirm prompts invite participation without scores, pressure, or negative feedback.
- [ ] Confirm all stories and rhymes are original summaries/titles; add no copied lyrics or story text.
- [ ] Confirm recording prompts are optional and clearly adult-led.
- [ ] Record reviewer role/date and any edits outside this public repository if identifying details are private.

## Recording checklist for future family audio

- [ ] Obtain the participating adult's permission; parent controls recording and deletion.
- [ ] Record in a quiet room with a short, warm voice sample; avoid names, locations, school details, or other identifiers.
- [ ] Keep clips short, listen back, and delete unusable takes promptly.
- [ ] Verify playback offline on the target device and confirm no clip is uploaded or included in a backup.
